How the view maps to the game

  • A building per premises. The spare room is a house, business units are clad units with stairs, office floors and the office building are towers with a lift. Moving day empties one building and fills the next.
  • A desk is a desk. Desks cap on-site headcount, as they do now. Free desks are drawn empty and labelled, so you can see when to move.
  • Floors fill from the ground up, in hire order. Your desk is the first one on the ground floor, where you do desk jobs.
  • Monitors show the contract: code typing in the contract's language, then a ✓ (with the pay) or a ✗. Each person's SLOC floats up as they write (item 20).
  • The interview room is on the ground floor, by the way in. Applicants sit and wait with their CV for a while, then give up and leave. Tap one to hire them; with no free desk they can't start.
  • Breaks. Now and then someone on a contract gets up for a break. Their contract stops while they're away (the monitor shows it paused), so breaks cost a little speed. Each floor has its own break room.
  • The bench is someone at their desk with no contract: a screensaver on the monitor.
  • Meeting rooms on the upper floors are where managers hold their stand-ups.
  • A stuck graduate flashes their screen (item 4c); tap them to help.
  • Hires walk from the interview room to their desk, taking the stairs or the lift to their floor; leavers go the same way.
  • Later: a floor per team once managers lead teams, an AI floor with no desks for item 17f, and a campus of buildings for item 18.

The drawing sits beside Ltd's text panels and doesn't replace them, so the game still plays on a phone and with a screen reader. Nothing is drawn while the tab is hidden.
